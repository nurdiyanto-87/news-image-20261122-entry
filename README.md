Entry for the [make.fun competition `news-image-20261122`](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122): one image that
sums up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, pure standard library only) draws a newspaper
front page — masthead naming the date, six story panels with hand-drawn
vector pictograms and short labels, and a footer ticker of further
headlines — and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(1024×1024 PNG, deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output).

## The six stories pictured

1. **Gulf on edge** — Iran says it downed two US drones over the Strait
   of Hormuz, as tankers are hit off Oman and Bahrain probes a Patriot
   interceptor crash. Washington has not confirmed any drone losses.
2. **Shenzhen summit** — Trump, Xi and Putin meet at the APEC summit.
   Trump calls the talks momentous; Zelenskyy warns on Ukraine.
3. **COP31 nears a deal** — Climate talks in Antalya close to a final
   pact after a row over fossil fuels pushes the summit into overtime.
4. **Baltic blaze** — Drones hit a Russian cargo ship in the Baltic Sea.
   The vessel catches fire; Finland tightens security.
5. **Boat tragedy in Nigeria** — 24 people die when a boat capsizes on
   its way to a funeral. Rescuers search the river for the missing.
6. **Estonia shuts border** — Tallinn will close its frontier with
   Russia. Moscow decries a new Iron Curtain as tensions rise in Europe.

## Also today (footer ticker)

Mexico arrests cartel chief "El Chapo Isidro" (11 killed in Sinaloa) ·

```sh

```
